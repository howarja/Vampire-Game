Vampire Game (Blood of the Host)
Made for the Brackeys Game Jam 2026.2
Concept: Welcome to the greatest party hosted on the block. You've been hired as an investigator by the host to protect people from vampires. Did I mentioned vampires? Yes the world is infested with creatures called "vampires". Will the party and host succumb first, or will you find the vampires before the party ends.

Developed in godot by a team of five. Used git for collaboration. Written in GDscript, shaders in GDSL.

Enviromental and character art were created from real photos taken by us.

Most sfx are made entirely by us, several were sourced from freesound.org https://freesound.org/people/skradz/sounds/176611/ https://freesound.org/people/dossantosbarbosa/sounds/482295/ https://freesound.org/people/pagancow/sounds/15419/ https://freesound.org/people/Greg_Surr/sounds/590171/ https://freesound.org/people/JordanWoodland/sounds/407450/ https://freesound.org/people/TheBuilder15/sounds/367050/ https://freesound.org/people/doctorphil/sounds/536770/
